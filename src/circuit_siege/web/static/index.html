    How to play

    1. Take turns placing a node on any empty cell. You are blue, the AI is red.
    2. Flank to capture. If your new node and another of your nodes trap an unbroken line of enemy nodes between them, in any of the 8 directions, that whole line flips to you.
      →
    3. Cores are worth 3 points. The five chip-shaped cells in a plus shape. Every other cell is worth 1.
    4. The game ends when the board is full (49 moves). There are 59 points in total, so 30 wins and a draw is impossible.

    Tips

    • Corners can never be captured. Edges are hard to flank.
    • Hover a cell to preview its captures before you commit.
    • A big capture can leave a line open for an even bigger recapture.
